Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aquarium Cafe & Restaurant ("we", "us", or "the restaurant") handles information submitted through this website and its online ordering features.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, phone number and email address when you create a customer account.
  • Delivery information: name, phone number, detailed delivery address, optional Google Maps location link, delivery zone/sub-zone and optional order notes.
  • Order information: items ordered, quantities, prices, discounts, delivery fee, VAT, payment method and order status/history.
  • Loyalty information: points earned, redeemed and related transaction history when the loyalty feature is enabled.
  • Reviews: the name, rating and review text you choose to submit. Reviews are subject to restaurant approval before publication.
  • Notification information: if you explicitly enable order notifications, the website may store the information needed to deliver those notifications.

2. Location

The website does not continuously track your location. If you choose the "Use my location" option during checkout and grant browser permission, your device provides a location that is converted into a Google Maps link for the delivery order. You can instead enter a Maps link yourself or leave this optional field empty.

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage customer accounts.
  • To receive, prepare, deliver and track orders.
  • To contact you about an order when necessary.
  • To apply discounts, coupons and loyalty points.
  • To display approved customer reviews.
  • To provide order-status notifications when enabled.
  • To maintain, secure and operate the website.

4. Service providers

The website uses third-party technical services to operate its features, including Supabase for authentication, database and related application services, and Google Maps links when a Maps location is provided. Those services may process information as required to provide their respective services and are governed by their own policies.

5. Payments

The current website records the payment method selected for an order, such as cash on delivery or card on delivery. The website does not ask you to enter full card numbers into its checkout form.

6. Data retention

We retain account, order, review and loyalty information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the restaurant's services, maintain records and handle customer requests, subject to applicable requirements.

7. Your choices

You may choose not to create an account and may place an order as a guest where guest ordering is available. Optional Maps location and notification features can be declined. If you want to ask about, correct or request deletion of information associated with your account or orders, contact us using the details below.
